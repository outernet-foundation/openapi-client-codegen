# design/

Pre-implementation ADRs and their execution plans — the in-flux tier, not present truth
about this repo. Nothing here is authoritative about shipped code; when a design is built,
its hardened parts fold into the co-located `AGENTS.md` and the note stops being
load-bearing. Records here may go stale without that being a defect. Each ADR rules its
decisions up front; once ruled, the open-questions section is deleted and the ruling is
recorded as a decision.
